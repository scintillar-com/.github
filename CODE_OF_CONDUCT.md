# Code of Conduct

We want Scintillar to be a welcoming community. Everyone who contributes to, maintains or uses its tools is expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be patient and collaborative, remembering that everyone here contributes in their free time.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but isn't limited to:

- Harassment, intimidation or bullying.
- Discriminatory comments about race, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, religion or any other personal characteristic.
- Threats of violence or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email **conduct@scintillar.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this Code of Conduct. Depending on the case, consequences include:

- A warning.
- Removing comments, issues or contributions.
- A temporary or permanent ban from the organization's spaces.
- Reporting to the relevant authorities in extreme cases.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every space related to Scintillar, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and social media channels associated with the community.
